# Requirements Document: Healthcare Disease Risk Prediction System

## Introduction

This document specifies the requirements for a healthcare AI system that predicts disease risks using patient data. The system analyzes patient information including age, medical history, symptoms, and lifestyle factors to provide early warnings, support clinical decision-making through dashboards, ensure secure data storage compliant with healthcare regulations, and generate AI-based recommendations for preventive care.

## Glossary

- **Risk_Prediction_Engine**: The AI component that analyzes patient data and calculates disease risk scores
